# Stay in scope

Do what was asked. Nothing more.

- Build exactly the requested change.
- See an unrelated improvement? Propose it in text. Don't commit it.
- No opportunistic refactors, renames, or "while I was here" edits inside a scoped task.
- Scope creep hides the real change and drifts from intent.
