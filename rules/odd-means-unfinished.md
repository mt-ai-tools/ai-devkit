# Odd means unfinished

Where the only way forward is odd — an exception, a workaround, a dependency
running the wrong way, a rule that would need bending — the oddness is the
finding.

- Say what you would have had to accept, and stop there. That sentence is
  worth more than the thing you would have built around it.
- "It's only here", "we can revisit it later", "the alternative is worse" are
  the three ways this gets past a reader. Each of them is the moment to look
  again, not the reason to proceed.
- The cleaner shape usually exists and has not been seen yet. Two of the
  hardest parts of a design are often one part in the wrong place.
- An exception written into a rule is the same thing wearing a permit. Where
  the exception is real, the rule was drawn wrong and the rule changes.
- This does not forbid a trade made knowingly. A cost weighed and recorded is
  a decision; a cost noticed and stepped over is the debt this rule is about.
