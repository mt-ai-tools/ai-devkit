description: An independent second reading of one finding — a fresh advisor works it cold, then is sent the proposal
argument-hint: [the finding — its proposed fix is kept back until the advisor has answered]
---

Start a fresh advisor agent on the finding below. Hand it the claim alone —
what is said to be wrong, where it sits, the law it was raised under — and
the folders the rules and the conventions live in. Leave out the proposed
fix, any recommendation, and which way you lean, even where they arrived
with the finding: the advisor runs in its own context precisely so that
none of this one reaches it, and a sentence of it in the hand-off undoes
that.

When it reports, send the proposal to that same advisor, and relay its
second report whole. Where there is no proposal, relay the first.
