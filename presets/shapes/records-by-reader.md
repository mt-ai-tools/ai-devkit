# Records by reader

- Every record is kept for one reader: the people who run the system, the people who serve its users, or the product. Its place is chosen by that reader.
- Every kind of record states how long it is kept. Nothing is kept without that.
- When the people who serve a user may need a record about that person, it is kept where those people read. It is never only in a place for the people who run the system.
- No block keeps a store of events of its own beside the shared places.
- When an event belongs in several places, one piece writes all of them from the same values.
- A record kept for the product counts people but never says who they are.
- When an outside service keeps its own log of people, that log is switched off. Where it cannot be, it is named wherever people are told what is kept.
- Every place a record lands is reached through one front, guarded by reader, with at least a summary page for each place.
