# Reproduce before reporting

- Alone means the one part, the one call, nothing else moving.
- Where it will not reproduce, say what was seen and that it did not
  repeat — and name nothing as the reason.
- Whatever else might have been moving belongs in the report, not in the
  correction afterwards: the reader deciding what to do needs to know the
  ground was uncertain.
- This binds a claim about the tools exactly as it binds one about the
  code.
- A finding that waits for a second look costs minutes. A wrong one is
  acted on, and the real fault stays.
