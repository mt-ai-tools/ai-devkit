# The challenge ladder

The operator reads one thing: whether a recommendation holds under
challenge. The same answer, held with reasons, earns trust; an answer
that keeps moving means the agent is not sure and must dig further.

1. The agent's first recommendation.
2. The standing test:
   > What is the clean way? What is the consistent way? What would big
   > companies do? No workarounds, no dirty stuff. Today's work is always
   > cheaper than tomorrow's.
3. Then:
   > Are you sure?

Each challenge the stand-in sends is the quote under its rung, word for
word.

The same option named from the same list all three times holds. A
changed answer is not held: it goes to the operator, beside a cold
second reading by another model that never saw the exchange.
