# The challenge ladder

The operator reads one thing: whether a recommendation holds under
challenge. The same answer, held with reasons, earns trust; an answer
that keeps moving means the agent is not sure and must dig further.

1. The agent's first recommendation.
2. `standing-test` — the standing test:
   > What is the clean way? What is the consistent way? What would big
   > companies do? No workarounds, no dirty stuff. Today's work is always
   > cheaper than tomorrow's.
3. `are-you-sure` — then:
   > Are you sure?

The same option chosen all three times holds, however the agent words
it. A changed answer is never held, whatever follows: it is taken a
bigger look around, then asked "are you sure?" once more, in the same
words. If the answer moves again, it goes to the operator beside a cold
second reading by another model that never saw the exchange. If it
holds, it goes to the operator without one, told it moved and then held.

## Before the operator is reached

- `bigger-look` — when the answer moved, before "are you sure?" is
  asked once more:
  > Since you are not sure, take a bigger look around. Look for the
  > clean and consistent way, expect growth, consider what big
  > companies would do, search the web, and ask questions if needed.

Each message the stand-in sends is the quote under its name, word for
word.
