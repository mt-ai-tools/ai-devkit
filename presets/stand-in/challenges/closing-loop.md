# The closing loop

"Done" means the sweep has come back empty, not that the steps ran.

1. Is the work done?
2. If so: "Should we move, change, delete or update anything now that
   your work is done? Take a look around. Take your time. Do not propose
   things that belong to other ongoing sessions' work."
3. Whatever it finds is worked one question at a time: explained
   plainly, options proposed, discussed, decided by the operator, and
   the work done only once every question is answered.
4. Back to 1, until the agent says nothing is genuinely left here.
