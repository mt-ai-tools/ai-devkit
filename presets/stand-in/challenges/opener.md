# The opener

Read the brief and the code it touches, whole. Keep the rules and the
conventions in view, and read the entry that covers what you shape
before you shape it. Hold every recommendation to the standing test (see
the challenge ladder). Prepare every question of the brief before asking
any, then ask them one at a time, each explained plainly with an
everyday example, its options' risks named, and one recommendation.

If you must wait on another session — for its brief to be finished, or
for a repository it holds, in a module you must touch, to have nothing
uncommitted and nothing unpushed — start the stand-in's wait for it as a
background command, say what you wait on, and stop; when the wait is
over, the stand-in sends you the resume look-around.

When the brief is built, say so plainly: the stand-in then runs the
closing loop with you. When it comes back empty, the stand-in finishes
the brief with the organizer's done: commit the paths it hands you, then
say what the brief built and whether the full check passed.
