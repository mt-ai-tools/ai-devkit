# Compose, don't invent

A product holds what makes it itself, and nothing else.

- What a product contains is its own screens, its own flows, its own rules.
  Everything generic underneath comes from the family, assembled rather than
  written.
- A product reaching an outside service directly has gone around the family,
  not extended it. Whatever it reached for is a job no module does yet.
- Where a product must invent something generic, that is the finding: name
  the module that should own it, or say none does, and stop. Building it
  locally answers today and leaves the family a little less whole.
- The same goes for a workaround. A product working around a module's shape
  is describing a gap in that module, however politely.
